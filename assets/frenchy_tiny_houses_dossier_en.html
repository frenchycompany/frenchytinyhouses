The Concept

— 02 / 07

A Tiny House optimised for Airbnb, eco-equipped, installed on a private landowner's property, fully managed end-to-end by Frenchy Conciergerie. Floor area: 18 m².

The target: the landowner

Frenchy Tiny Houses does not target campsite operators, institutional investors, or mini-village type developments (which qualify as Open-Air Hospitality and fall under a specific regime). Our target is the private owner of a primary or secondary residence, with an underused back garden.

75 m² Minimum footprint
500 m² Average target plot size
< 50 m From the access road

Concretely, the 75 m² footprint (~ 18 m² for the module + clearance, deck and access) represents only a fraction of a standard plot. The deciding criterion is not the plot's size, but its accessibility: flat ground, reachable by delivery vehicles, less than 50 m from the road. Geography matters less than expected — profitability depends more on management quality than absolute location.

Why 18 m²

This surface is not arbitrary: it simultaneously optimises five economic and administrative parameters.

  • Lower costs on acquisition and fit-out.
  • Fast installation — one month after site preparation.
  • Simplified maintenance over the lifetime of the module.
  • Excellent yield per rented m².
  • Simplified administrative procedures, leveraging the 20 m² threshold of French regulation.

What Frenchy Tiny Houses handles

The entire journey, from investment decision to monthly cashflow: upfront advisory, installation, administrative support, and full rental management via Frenchy Conciergerie. The owner provides the land and collects monthly net income — they touch nothing else.

Why Invest Now

— Summary

Three rational reasons, one execution argument, one eco-responsible direction.

01

Industrial risk is removed

A Tiny House is already installed and in operation. The Everbox module is a proven product, 10-year warranty, delivered within a month. The project does not require funding uncertain R&D — only the scaling-up of an already demonstrated model.

02

Operations already exist

Frenchy Conciergerie manages 20+ properties, holds the French Carte T and G real estate licences, masters Airbnb / Booking automation, and already operates the first Tiny House. The receiving structure is in place — each sale immediately feeds an existing operational business.

03

The market is buoyant & eco-conscious

Eco-responsible unique stays are one of the fastest-growing Airbnb segments. Tiny Houses, solar panels, rainwater harvesting: a cocktail of commercial arguments that justifies a higher average price. No French player has yet industrialised this turnkey offer.

The execution argument

Frenchy Tiny Houses is not a project run by a founder looking for experience. It is the natural extension of an already-built entrepreneurial ecosystem, with a validated product, an operational team, and an existing customer base.

The investor is not funding a promise — they are funding the acceleration of mechanics that already work. That is precisely what sets this dossier apart from classic early-stage projects: every euro invested goes into a machine that is already running, not one to be built.

Long-term ambition

Beyond short-term return on investment, Frenchy Tiny Houses is built as a transferable and scalable asset: a recurring revenue stream grounded in long-term management contracts, backed by a fleet of eco-equipped physical assets. The most natural exit profile at a 5-7 year horizon is a sale to a player in open-air hospitality, unique tourism, or property concierge management.
